## What changed for a negotiating team

- Each community draft now pairs its original precedents with a regional decision or operating example, a link to evidence, and a specific request to bring to a meeting. The Adamstown example includes the decision to refuse an agreement.
- The common worksheet now separates a due amount, a payment, a recipient and proof of completion. Memphis makes the distinction concrete: a budget for home repairs is not a count of repaired homes.
- All four community drafts and both policy drafts now have downloadable, searchable PDFs with clickable public sources. Community packets contain only the chosen setting plus the shared terms, pause guidance and sources; the editable versions remain available.
- Before a real negotiation, field-test a filled-in worksheet with a resident representative and a public finance officer. Independent verification of local disbursements and household outcomes remains a reporting priority.
