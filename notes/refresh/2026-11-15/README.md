**What we verified.** The [source ledger](sources.csv) ties 921 citations to their records. Of 796 distinct URLs, 726 returned readable content to the automated check; 70 were blocked, unreadable or otherwise inconclusive, and none returned a confirmed 404/410. We checked main citations, official campus and company pages, nested resources and federal coordinate sources. For operators without a dedicated impact page we checked a linked company statement or, if none was available, a linked site citation; a site citation does not establish a company-wide program. When direct site or company evidence was unreadable, we also tried up to two distinct linked claim sources where available.

The [record ledger](records.csv) reports lexical checks: city and operator names for projects, a site name for federal references, company identity, and individual policy terms across retrieved sources. A name or term match establishes that the words appear; it does not certify every field, legal status, delivered benefit or financial projection. Unmatched paraphrases, translations and scans need review. Of 215 policies, all stored terms matched text for 176, some matched for eight, none matched for 20, and no text was machine-readable for 11. The [28 term reviews](policy-triage.csv) distinguish earlier reviews, fresh corroboration, corrected facts and a remaining Georgia legislative follow-up.

We followed up on [all 19 records without machine-readable evidence](follow-up.csv). Public page text or OCR of the cited document supported a specific fact for 18. The Prologis announcement was visible in its search index, but we could not verify its full text. We left that limitation in the ledger. These follow-ups do not change the recorded HTTP outcomes.

**Announcements and corrections.**

- [Salem Township](https://www.amazoninnovationinpa.com/salem-township-innovation-campus/) published a $22 million agreement summary. It does not report payments. Removed Pennsylvania-wide investment and job projections from campus totals and separated the corresponding statewide claims.
- [OpenAI and SB Energy](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/milam-county-data-center-community-compact.pdf) presented the Milam County compact on November 9. The operators promise updates to the court at least four times a year, an annual public report, and acknowledgment of inquiries within a week. This is not a finding of county adoption or delivery.
- [Spartanburg County](https://www.spartanburgcounty.gov/1452/Data-Center-Information-Resources) advanced separate AIB ($12.6 million) and 300 Jones Road ($9 million) agreements on second reading. Each payment schedule is conditional. Neither is recorded as approved or paid.
- [Washington’s revenue department](https://dor.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/2026_Legislative_Report.pdf) distinguishes replacement servers and refurbishment certificates from original equipment at newly built facilities. [Arkansas Act 548](https://arkleg.state.ar.us/Home/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2025R%2FPublic%2FACT548.pdf) measures large-center compensation over the two calendar years following the year operations begin.

The developer later described closed-loop cooling; the tracker no longer treats the reporting as proof of a blanket contractual ban on water cooling or a court decision on the merits.
- The [Google/SpaceX filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026041150/spacexagreementfwp.htm) describes fees for compute services. Removed the annualized fee from construction investment. The [Port Washington entry](https://vantage-dc.com/news/openai-oracle-and-vantage-data-centers-announce-stargate-data-center-site-in-wisconsin/) now cites the campus announcement instead of the nationwide Stargate launch.
